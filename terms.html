1. What Domos is

Domos is software for managing apartment buildings and rental properties: apartments and their shares, common expenses and contributions, tenants, notices, maintenance issues, and an optional read-only link to the building's bank account. It is provided by Andreas Malekkos, Cyprus ("we").

Our Privacy Notice explains how that data is handled. Data stays on your device unless you sign in to a Domos account, in which case it is also stored in that account.

3. Bank account link

Linking a bank account is optional. It uses Enable Banking Oy, a licensed account-information service provider; you approve access at your bank with your own credentials, the access is read-only, lasts at most 180 days, and you can revoke it at any time in Domos or at your bank. Domos never initiates payments or holds your banking credentials. You must only link accounts you are entitled to access.
