Privacy Notice

Domos — building & property management · last updated 16 September 2026

Domos is software used by property managers to run apartment buildings and rental properties: apartments, common expenses, contributions, tenants and the building's bank account. This notice explains what personal data Domos handles, why, and your rights.

Who is responsible

Domos is operated by Andreas Malekkos, Cyprus. For anything about your data write to andreasapps@outlook.com. When a property-management company uses Domos for the buildings it manages, that company is the controller of the data about its buildings and residents, and Domos processes it on the company's instructions.

What data Domos handles

  • Building and apartment records — apartment numbers, sizes, shares of common expenses, owners' and tenants' names and contact details, notices sent, maintenance issues.
  • Money records — common expenses, contributions received, rent bills and payments.
  • Bank account data — when you choose to link a building's bank account, Domos reads the account's balance and transactions (payer name, IBAN, reference, amount, date) so that incoming payments can be matched to apartments. Access is read-only; Domos never initiates payments.

How the bank link works

Bank accounts are accessed through Enable Banking Oy, a licensed account-information service provider under PSD2. You are redirected to your bank, you approve read-only access there with your own bank credentials, and the bank tells Enable Banking which accounts you allowed. Domos never sees or stores your online-banking username, password or one-time codes. Access lasts at most 180 days and can be revoked at any time from Domos (building → Bank → Disconnect) or at your bank. Enable Banking's own notice is at enablebanking.com/privacy.

Where the data is kept

Domos is local-first: your data is stored on your own device (browser storage or the app's storage on your phone). If you sign in to a Domos account to use it on several devices, the same data is stored, encrypted in transit, in that account and nowhere else. Bank transactions fetched through the link are stored with the rest of the building's records so that matching and receipts keep working offline.

Why and on what basis

  • To run the building's accounts and notify residents — the legitimate interest of the building's management, or a contract with it.
  • To read a bank account — your explicit consent, given at the bank during the link and withdrawable at any time.

Who else sees it

Nobody, except: the bank and Enable Banking (to provide the account data you asked for), an e-mail or SMS provider when you send a notice to a resident, and, if you use a Domos account, the hosting provider that stores that account's data. Domos does not sell data, does not use it for advertising and does not profile anyone.

How long

For as long as you keep the building in Domos. Deleting a building deletes its apartments, expenses, payments, notices and issues; disconnecting a bank account deletes the link and stops all further access. You can also delete every record by resetting the app or your account.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and object to processing; you can also withdraw bank-access consent at any time. Write to andreasapps@outlook.com. You may complain to the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection of Cyprus (dataprotection.gov.cy).

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it and the current version is always at this address.
